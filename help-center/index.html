Getting Started

Yatrify is an AI-powered travel planning platform that helps you create personalized trip itineraries. Simply enter your destination, travel dates, and preferences, and Yatrify generates a detailed day-by-day plan tailored to your style.
After signing up, go to your Dashboard and click "Create New Plan." Enter your destination, travel dates, number of travelers, budget range, and any interests or preferences. Yatrify will generate a complete itinerary for you within seconds.
Yatrify offers a free tier that lets you create a limited number of trip plans. For unlimited plans and premium features, you can upgrade to one of our paid plans. Visit the Pricing section for details.
Yes, you need to create a free account to generate and save trip plans. You can sign up using your email address or through a social login provider. Creating an account takes just a few seconds.

Account & Profile

Click on your profile avatar in the top right corner of the page. From there you can update your name, profile picture, and other account details through your account settings.
Navigate to your profile settings by clicking your avatar. Select "Security" and then "Change Password." You will be asked to verify your current password before setting a new one. If you signed up via a social login provider, you can add a password through the same security settings.
Yes. If you wish to delete your account, please contact our support team. We will process your request and permanently remove your data in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Please note that this action is irreversible.

Billing & Plans

We accept all major credit and debit cards including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Payments are securely processed and your card details are never stored on our servers.
You can change your subscription plan at any time from your Dashboard under "Subscription" settings. When upgrading, the price difference is prorated. When downgrading, the change takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle.
If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can request a refund within 7 days of your payment. Please contact our support team with your account email and reason for the refund. Refunds are typically processed within 5-10 business days.
Go to your Dashboard, navigate to "Subscription" settings, and click "Cancel Subscription." You will continue to have access to premium features until the end of your current billing period. Your saved trip plans will remain accessible on the free tier.

Trip Planning

Yes, all generated itineraries are fully customizable. You can add, remove, or rearrange activities, change timings, and update any details to match your preferences. Your changes are saved automatically.
Open the trip plan you want to share and click the "Share" button. You can generate a shareable link to send to friends or family. You can also make your plan public so it appears in the Community Plans section for other travelers to discover.
Yes, Yatrify supports multi-destination trips. When creating a new plan, you can add multiple cities or locations. The AI will generate a connected itinerary with suggested travel routes between destinations.
Yatrify supports destinations worldwide. Whether you are planning a trip within your country or an international adventure, our AI can generate itineraries for virtually any location. Popular destinations include cities across Asia, Europe, the Americas, and more.

Community

Community Plans are trip itineraries shared publicly by other Yatrify users. You can browse these plans for inspiration, copy them to your own dashboard, and customize them to suit your needs. Visit the Community Plans page to explore.
Open your trip plan and look for the visibility settings. Toggle "Make Public" to share your itinerary with the Yatrify community. You can switch it back to private at any time. Public plans help other travelers discover great trip ideas.

Troubleshooting

Try refreshing the page first. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again. Make sure you have a stable internet connection. If the problem continues, please contact support with details about your browser and device.
Double-check that you are using the correct email address and password. If you forgot your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the sign-in page to reset it. If you signed up using a social login (Google, etc.), make sure you are using the same provider. If issues persist, reach out to our support team.
Try being more specific with your preferences when creating a plan. Include details like your interests (adventure, culture, food, relaxation), budget level, and pace preference. You can also regenerate the plan or manually edit individual activities to better suit your needs.
Yatrify works best on the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We recommend keeping your browser up to date for the best experience. Mobile browsers on iOS and Android are also fully supported.
